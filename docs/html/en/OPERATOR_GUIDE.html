Intake operator guide

Русский · Română · English

The goal is to sort a bag in minutes, not hours. Every item must be recorded as a “model · colour · size” variant with a barcode and a price.

Workstation setup

  • A laptop or PC running TexFlow (python run.py). For several workstations: python run.py --host 0.0.0.0, and the others open http://laptop-IP:5077.
  • A barcode scanner in keyboard mode with an Enter suffix. Most scanners use this mode by default.
  • A Zebra or TSC thermal printer with a 58×40 mm roll, or an office printer with A4 sheets.
  • A large table and boxes labelled by category: T-shirts, jeans, dresses…
  • Interface language: the RU / RO / EN switch in the header.

Step 1. Create the shipment

“Shipments” → “New shipment”.

Field Example Why
Number TR-2026-11-01 reference in documents
Supplier Istanbul Tekstil product cards
Country Turkey / China country of origin
Currency, rate USD × 17.8 converting purchase prices to local currency
Freight, Customs, Broker, Other 4500, 1200, 800, 0 in local currency, go into landed cost
Allocate costs by value expensive items carry more. “By weight” — if you paid per kg
Bags 4 create bags 1..4 right away (if there is no list)

Step 2. Upload the packing list

The supplier's list usually comes in Excel. Save it as CSV: “File → Save as → CSV (semicolon-separated)”.

Required columns (any order, extra columns are ignored):

Column Header variants
bag bag_no, bag, sac, мешок, çuval, 包
category category, categorie, категория, kategori, 类别 — code (TSH, JNS) or a reference name (Russian, Romanian or English)
description description, descriere, описание, açıklama, 品名
quantity (required) qty, cantitate, pcs, кол-во, adet, 数量
price per pc unit_price, price, preț, цена, fiyat, 单价
weight, kg weight_kg, weight, greutate, вес, ağırlık, 重量

The list is called rough: “T-shirts — 120 pcs” with no colours or sizes. That is enough. Colours and sizes appear during sorting, and the quantities are checked in step 4.

If a category is not recognised, you get a warning. Add such a category in “Reference data” and upload the list again.

Step 3. Sort the bag

Shipment page → “Sort” next to the bag.

The item already exists (e.g. a second batch of the same model): scan its label or barcode — +1 pc is added. The result appears under the scanner field: ✓ — received, ✗ — no such code.

New model:

  1. Lay the bag's contents out on the table by model. Within a model — by colour, within a colour — by size. This is the key technique: lay out first, then enter quantities, instead of entering items one by one.
  2. Fill in “New model”: the name (as it will appear on the label), the category (it determines the size scale and the markup) and, if known, the composition. The style number is generated (TSH0007). The purchase price is taken from the packing list by category and can be corrected.
  3. The matrix opens. Click the colours you need — each becomes a row. Colours are grouped as on the Zara website: White (white, off-white, ecru), Beige (beige, sand, camel), Blue (blue, navy, denim)…
  4. Enter quantities into the “colour × size” cells. Enter moves to the next cell, arrows move the cursor. Row and column totals update instantly.
  5. Ctrl+Enter or “Save”. The “Save and print labels” button opens printing for exactly this batch.

Mistake: in the intake log press × on the line, or enter a negative number (−2) in the matrix.

When the bag is empty — “Bag done”.

Step 4. Reconcile with the list

Shipment page, blocks “Reconciliation by category” and “Reconciliation by bag”:

  • match — all good;
  • shortage (red) — less arrived than listed: grounds for a claim to the supplier;
  • surplus — more arrived;
  • not in list — the category was not declared.

The list is rough, so a 2–5 % difference is usually normal.

Step 5. Prices

“Prices” on the shipment. For every model you see:

  • the purchase price in currency;
  • the landed cost in local currency, with duty and a share of freight, customs and broker;
  • the category markup;
  • the proposed price (already rounded: 189.90, 249.90…);
  • the actual markup after rounding.

You can type your own price in “Own price”. Then “Write prices to product cards”.

If a model's price must not change (promotion, negotiated price), lock it on the model card with “Lock price”. Recalculation does not touch a locked price.

Category markups are changed in “Reference data”. Rounding (step, .90/.99 ending) and VAT — in “Settings”.

Step 6. Labels

  • For the whole shipment or a bag — from the shipment page. As many labels as pieces received.
  • For a model — from the model card.
  • A single label — the “label” button next to a variant (e.g. to replace a damaged one).

Before printing you can adjust the number of copies on each line.

Choosing a format:

  • ZPL — for Zebra;
  • TSPL — for TSC;
  • PDF A4 — for sheets of self-adhesive labels. Print at 100% scale. “Start at cell” lets you finish a partly used sheet;
  • PDF roll — for printing through a driver or to a virtual printer.

Buttons: “Open / download”, “Download file”, “Print to printer” (to the address from settings).

The colour language on the label (Black / Negru / Чёрный) is chosen in “Settings”. Printer details — LABELS.en.md.

Step 7. Catalogue

“Catalogue” — model cards with colours, price and stock. Inside a card:

  • colours with codes and groups;
  • the size grid (struck through — out of stock);
  • the SKU and EAN table;
  • a print-card button.

Export CSV — the full list of variants (style no., colour, size, SKU, EAN, price, stock) for uploading to an online shop or POS. Colour and category names are in the current interface language.
