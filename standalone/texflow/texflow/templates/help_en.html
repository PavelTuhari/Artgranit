In short

  1. Shipment. “Shipments” menu → number, supplier, currency and rate to the local currency, costs in local currency. Allocation method: by value (expensive items carry more), per piece, or by bag weight.
  2. Packing list. CSV/TSV from Excel: bag; category; description; qty; price; weight. Headers in Russian, Romanian, English, Turkish and Chinese are understood. Unknown categories are highlighted.
  3. Sorting. “Sort” on a bag. Repeat items — with the scanner. New model — the “New model” form, then click colours (rows) and enter quantities per size (columns). Enter — next cell, Ctrl+Enter — save. Mistake — enter a negative number or delete the log line.
  4. Reconciliation. On the shipment page: by category and by bag — “shortage”, “surplus”, “not in list”.
  5. Prices. “Prices” → a proposal per model. You can type your own price; the price on the card can be locked (padlock), then recalculation leaves it alone.
  6. Labels. For the whole shipment, a bag, a model or one variant; quantity — by pieces received. Formats: ZPL (Zebra), TSPL (TSC), PDF A4, PDF roll.
